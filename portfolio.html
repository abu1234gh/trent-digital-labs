Evan's Smart Fix

Website development for a phone repair business.

Client project Evan's Smart Fix Phone repair website

The project

Evan's Smart Fix is a phone repair business that needed a dedicated online presence for its services.

Trent Digital Labs worked on the website to present repair information in an organised format and provide customers with an online route to enquire about repairs.

Visit the live website ↗

Work undertaken

  • Website design and development
  • Repair service information
  • Phone brand and model pages
  • Booking-related functionality
  • Shopping cart functionality
  • Payment integration work
  • Customer and business email workflows
Development technologies

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Supabase, SumUp and EmailJS were used in the project.

Project features and integrations are described according to the development work undertaken. Availability and operation of individual features may depend on the current live implementation and service configuration.

Our approach

From idea to website

Good website development starts with understanding what a business needs and turning those requirements into a practical online experience.

01. Understand

Learn about the business, its customers, services and goals before deciding how the website should work.

02. Build

Develop a website around the agreed requirements, with an emphasis on clarity, usability and consistent design.

03. Refine

Review the website, test its functionality and make agreed adjustments before launch.

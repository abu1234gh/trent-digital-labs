Ongoing costs

Know what you pay for.

The website build fee is separate from third-party costs and optional ongoing support. We will identify these in your quote before you approve the project.

Costs to consider

  • One-off website design and development
  • Domain registration and annual renewal
  • Optional maintenance or update support
  • Paid third-party tools, if required
  • Alternative hosting for advanced functionality

GitHub Pages can host eligible static websites without a separate hosting subscription, but domain registration and third-party services may still cost money.

Important: A final quote should state the deliverables, number of revisions, payment schedule, ownership and handover, delivery timeline, and any recurring fees. This page deliberately does not publish unconfirmed prices.
